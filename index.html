Haoxuan Zhang

Ph.D. student in Information Science (Data Science)
IDEA Lab, University of North Texas, Denton, Texas

  • haoxuanzhang@my.unt.edu
  • Google Scholar
  • OpenReview

I study how scientific innovation can be measured, and how large language models can take part in evaluating science: as reviewers, as collaborators, and as tools for knowledge discovery.

I am a member of the Intelligent Data Engineering and Analytics (IDEA) Lab, advised by Prof. Haihua Chen and Prof. Ting Xiao. My work combines natural language processing, machine learning, and computational scientometrics, with a focus on automated scientific peer review.
